# The couch routine

How a session goes once everything is set up. Nothing here needs you to be at the PC.

## Start

1. The PC is on. It can be locked, with the monitors off.
2. On the Thor, open **DS Forever** and choose **Streaming**.
3. If the PC is locked, the top screen shows the Windows lock screen. Tap the top screen once, press **Keyboard** on
   the bottom screen, and type your PIN or password.
4. WoW Forever has already been started on the PC. Pick your character and play.

About 25 seconds in, the PC's monitors go dark. The sound comes out of the Thor, and the PC stays quiet.

## End

In the world:

1. Tap **Start**. WoW's own Game Menu opens on the top screen.
2. Touch **Log Out** on the top screen with a finger. A touch on the top screen is a mouse click on the PC.
   The D-pad and sticks do not move through this menu.
3. The Thor shows "Waiting for the game". On the bottom screen: **Menu > Exit game**.

Then put the Thor to sleep. Within about half a minute the PC closes WoW, turns the monitors back on, restores its
sound, and locks itself.

Simply backing out of DS Forever at any point has the same effect on the PC. It is less tidy inside the game: outdoors
your character stays in the world for the logout timer.

## Things that look wrong but are not

- **"Starting WoW on your PC / Waiting for the world"** after you quit WoW from its menu. It is only the app's waiting
  screen. WoW is not restarted. Use Menu > Exit game or Menu > Switch mode.
- **The PC's mouse pointer jumps to the top of the screen** about once a second during a session. The companion drives
  the game that way. It stops when the session ends. Do not use the PC while someone is playing on the Thor.
- **"The game refused it"** on the companion's own Game Menu page. WoW does not let add-ons quit the game. Use WoW's
  menu as described above.
- **A dialog saying an add-on "has been blocked"** with Disable and Ignore. Choose Ignore. The optional
  NoAddonBlockedPopup add-on removes this dialog.
- **A stray finger on the top screen moves the PC's mouse.** The top screen is a touchpad for the PC while streaming.

## The other path: Artemis and Apollo

For the whole desktop or any other game, open **Artemis** on the Thor and pick the PC. It also shows the lock screen,
and you sign in the same way.

End an Artemis session with **quit** (long-press the PC or the app in Artemis, then Quit), not by just closing the app.
A stream that is only disconnected stays paused on the PC. If Apollo was set to change the display layout for the
stream, the PC keeps that layout until the app is quit.

## Looking after the two OLED screens

A static bottom screen for hours is how OLED burn-in starts. Sleep the Thor when you stop playing, and do not leave it
sitting on the waiting screen.
